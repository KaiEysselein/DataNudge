ANDROID MOBILE-DATA REMINDER

Know when selected apps open on mobile data.

Choose apps such as YouTube, Netflix, or Spotify. DataNudge reminds you when one opens while your phone is connected through mobile data.

By downloading and using DataNudge, you acknowledge that the app is an informational reminder only, may use device resources and battery, and does not prevent mobile-data charges. Read the Disclaimer and Terms of Use.

FEATURES

A simple nudge before mobile data disappears.

App monitoring

Choose the high-data apps that matter to you.

Connection awareness

See whether the phone is on Wi-Fi, mobile data, VPN, or offline.

Clear reminders

Receive a clear reminder when a selected app opens on mobile data.

Local settings

Your selections and preferences are stored locally on your device.

HOW IT WORKS

Set it once, then let it watch quietly.

  1. Grant the required Android permissions.
  2. Select the apps you want to monitor.
  3. Start background monitoring.
  4. Get reminded when a selected app opens on mobile data.
